# Blackwish archive provenance

## Purpose and coverage

This archive preserves selected completed Blackwish research and reference deliverables under the direction of creator Brandon Groesbeck. It is a documented partial backup, not a representation that the entire Blackwish project or its canonical record is present. The complete Working Doctrine version 12 and source ledger version 12 are not verified as included; excerpts and references are not substitutes for those originals. Software is excluded.

## Contributions and attribution

Brandon Groesbeck supplies the project direction, requirements and decisions recorded in the work. AI assistants contributed research assistance, comparisons, synthesis, examples, drafts, document production and review. Individual documents and associated records govern any more specific claim about who supplied a passage, decision, source or implementation. This archive does not assert that every sentence or line of code was personally written by Brandon Groesbeck, nor does it reconstruct unsupported percentages of human and AI contribution.

Third-party research, quotations, standards, documentation and creative works remain attributable to their named sources. Citation is not a transfer of ownership or an automatic permission to redistribute a source in full. Raw downloaded source collections and third-party software libraries are not included by default.

## Identity and preservation

The publication manifest records each included file's public relative path, title, byte count and SHA-256 checksum. A checksum supports identification of a particular sequence of bytes. It does not establish authorship, truth, legal ownership, the date of original creation or permission to reuse a work.

Where an original was eligible for publication without alteration, its archived bytes should remain unchanged. A privacy-cleaned derivative must have its own checksum and a description of the removals or replacements. It must not masquerade as the unchanged original. Private inventories, local development paths, account identifiers, raw conversations, credentials and internal assistant notes are not public provenance artifacts.

## Research and approval status

Document preparation, internal review, user ratification, implementation authorization and deployment are distinct events. A completed report may still contain unratified proposals. Earlier documents can retain their original status language even when a later, separate addendum records a bounded approval. Read the addendum's subject, date and exclusions rather than treating approval as a blanket status.

Approval receipts record the conversation evidence available when they were prepared. Unless expressly established otherwise, they are project records rather than independently authenticated digital signatures. An approval to preserve or publish a document does not ratify its every recommendation, prove its conclusions or authorize operational changes.

## What the archive does not establish

The existence of an archive, repository timestamp or checksum is not proof of sole authorship, originality, intellectual-property ownership or legal priority. The documents preserve dated research and uncertainty; this archival review is not a fresh verification that every cited fact remains current. Historical test results retain their stated environments and scope. Unperformed browser, device, human-participant or live-system tests do not become completed through archival publication.

# Blackwish research and reference archive

**Creator and project director: Brandon Groesbeck.**

This collection preserves selected completed Blackwish research, reference documents, design records and narrowly scoped approval addenda. It provides a readable backup of the included work and records its sources and status.

**This is a research and reference archive, not a complete canonical Blackwish backup.** The complete Working Doctrine version 12 and source ledger version 12 have not been verified as included. References to those records in a report do not substitute for their original files. Software, live services and unpublished development material are excluded from this release.

Browse the [document index](INDEX.md) or inspect the [file manifest](MANIFEST.json).

## Read the status with the document

The archive manifest identifies included files and their SHA-256 checksums. Each document's own date, evidence limits and status remain part of the record. A completed research report may contain proposals that have not been ratified. A later approval addendum applies only to its named material and stated scope; it does not silently approve future proposals, certify results or authorize deployment.

Historical originals are preserved where suitable for release. Any privacy-cleaned derivative must be labeled as a derivative and recorded separately from its original. Public reading or reuse does not grant repository write, administration or deployment access.
